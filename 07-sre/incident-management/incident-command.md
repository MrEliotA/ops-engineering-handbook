# Incident Command

Separate roles during significant incidents: incident commander, operations/technical lead, communications, and scribe when staffing permits. Maintain a timestamped decision log.

The IC owns priorities and coordination, not every shell command. Define stop conditions for risky remediation and preserve a known rollback path.
